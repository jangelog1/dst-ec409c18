Around the Clubhouse

🧿Briefing — latest Sox news

Updated Monday, August 3, 2026 · morning run · clubhouse + transactions roundup. A loud weekend on the Gulf Coast. The Sox closed out Tampa Bay with a 9–1 win Sunday to take the series 2-1 from the team with the best record in the American League — and they did it hours after making the biggest deadline move of the Chris Getz era. Late Saturday night, Chicago acquired RHP Luis Castillo from Seattle for closer Seranthony Domínguez, Double-A catching prospect Boston Smith and Triple-A OF Nolan Jones. Then overnight Sunday into Monday they added bullpen help, landing RHP Huascar Brazobán from the Mets for minor-league arms Gabe Davis (No. 18 org prospect) and Zach Franklin. On the field Sunday, Anthony Kay (8-5) spun 6⅓ scoreless innings on seven hits with one walk and three strikeouts. Andrew Benintendi hit his 12th homer, Everson Pereira had a two-run double and his fourth homer, and Miguel Vargas capped it with a 413-foot shot to left. Rays starter Griffin Jax (6-9) was charged with three runs, two of which scored on his own fielding error and a wild pitch in the first. Chicago is 58-52, first in the AL Central by 3½ games over Minnesota — the club entered August in first place for the first time since 2021. Today is an off day; the deadline hits at 5 PM CT, and the road trip resumes Tuesday at Fenway Park.

  • TradeRHP Luis Castillo acquired from Seattle (reported late Sat Aug 1) for RHP Seranthony Domínguez, C Boston Smith (Double-A Birmingham) and OF Nolan Jones (Triple-A Charlotte). Castillo, 33, is a three-time All-Star signed through 2027 at $22.75M per year with a $25M vesting option for 2028. He is 3-9 with a career-worst 5.06 ERA in 20 appearances (17 starts) this year but owns a 3.65 career ERA and a 2.25 ERA over 28 postseason innings. Chicago takes on the full remaining salary; Seattle absorbs the rest of Domínguez's two-year, $20M deal.
  • TradeRHP Huascar Brazobán acquired from the Mets (overnight Sun–Mon) for RHP Gabe Davis and RHP Zach Franklin. Brazobán, 36, is having his best season — 2.61 ERA, 47 K in 51⅔ IP — and is under team control through 2029. Davis, 22, was the club's No. 18 prospect (2025 fifth-rounder, recently promoted to Double-A); Franklin, 27, had five saves at Charlotte.

A statement finish. Chicago got on the board in the first without a hit leaving the infield — Benintendi came around on a Griffin Jax fielding error, and Colson Montgomery scored from third on what was officially scored a Jax wild pitch. Anthony Kay handled the rest, working 6⅓ scoreless on seven hits and a single walk to move to 8-5. Benintendi launched his 12th homer 378 feet over the right-center wall in the fourth, Everson Pereira doubled home two in the sixth and added his fourth homer of the year in the ninth, and Miguel Vargas closed the scoring with a 413-footer into the left-field seats. Duncan Davitt gave up the only Tampa Bay run — a Ben Williamson solo shot — on his first pitch of the ninth. The Sox take two of three from the team with the best record in the American League, less than 24 hours after trading for Luis Castillo. During the game, the Rays countered by acquiring Freddy Peralta from the Mets.
